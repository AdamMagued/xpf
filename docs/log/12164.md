# Issue #12164 — yield unready non-preempt incumbents to a healthier peer

## Why

The dataplane-arm monitor deliberately leaves the local RG at weight 1 so a node without a peer does not resign and lose its management/VIP path. But the non-preempt election also kept an incumbent primary at weight 1, even while its transit gate was closed and a peer at weight 255 could forward. The canonical HA example leaves `preempt` unset, so enabling preempt only in that example would not fix other non-preempt clusters.

## What

- In non-preempt mode, a data-RG incumbent (RG0 excluded) with positive
  reserved dataplane-arm debt yields only to a live peer under the current
  echo lease, with greater raw weight that wins receiver ordering (higher
  effective priority, or an effective tie won by the lower peer node ID).
  The arm-debt floor remains 1 for standalone eligibility; ordinary monitor
  debt and higher-weight peers that lose the ordering do not bypass
  non-preempt. The incumbent stays stable when the peer loses the effective
  tie.
- No global preempt default or canonical config change was made. The exception is specific to the fail-closed dataplane-arm sentinel.
- Two-manager heartbeat coverage verifies the unready incumbent yields to the weight-255 peer, then stays secondary after recovery (non-preempt remains in force). Controls verify standalone primary retention and unchanged behavior for ordinary monitor debt.
- A bounded daemon fixture removes the final kernel-proven XDP link and verifies the transit knobs/barrier close, arm debt is installed, and standalone primary ownership remains.

## Fold (Astra round-1: F1 + F2 + F3 + F4 + F5)

- **F1 (ownerless yield — fixed)**: the incumbent yield now requires a live
 echo lease (session == LocalProcessToken, sequence > 0, lease unexpired)
 on the peer frame. A stale/replayed readiness or a peer that failed after
 announcing no longer strands the RG ownerless: owner stays primary when
 the peer cannot prove bidirectionality, and yield + receiver-takeover are
 a symmetric pair (either both move or neither does).
- **F2 (yield/retake oscillation — fixed)**: the pre-fix code yielded on a
 bare reads of a heartbeat pair, so a stable fault flapped ownership every
 round. The echo-gated yield + receiver-side takeover rule (ready + no
 local debt + localWeight > peerWeight + peer at/below arm floor) make the
 handoff monotonic: one transition per fault, plus at most one bounded
 dual-active resolution.
- **F3 (committed-incumbent blackhole — fixed)**: the yield rule clears a
 stale transferCommitRolePinned=secondary pin on the receiver side, so a
 committed new owner at the arm floor hands back to the healthy old owner
 instead of blackholing behind its pin.
- **F4 (marker-only yield — fixed)**: the yield keys on
 dataplaneArmDebtLocked() > 0 (authoritative monitor weight), not on
 MonitorFails marker presence — a zero-cost configured marker or a
 retained marker without debt no longer yields. New helper documents the
 marker-vs-debt distinction.
- **F5 (flaky regression — fixed)**: the flake was an exact-count wire
 assertion broken by an always-on reserved record plus a
 completion-barrier gap; the fold emits no wire record at all (single-file
 election.go change), so the wire shape is unchanged for quiescent RGs.

An earlier fold iteration built a two-way handshake (reserved arm-debt
monitor record + takeover-confirmation echo sequence); reviewer probes
showed the handshake both stalled the canonical handoff (receiver never
promoted on synthetic peer groups) and left stale-announcement races. It
was removed in favor of the echo-lease gate + symmetric weight rule above.
No wire-format change; no heartbeat/manager/group_state changes.

## Final design summary for squash body

- **Ordering-gated yield:** A primary on a data RG (RG0 excluded) with
  positive dataplane-arm debt yields only to a live peer with greater raw
  weight that wins receiver ordering (effective priority, then node ID),
  under a current echo lease. The committed-primary pin uses the same gate.
- **Observed-primary weight-1 arm:** A SECONDARY/weight-1 peer is a yielded
  shape only after PRIMARY was observed in that peer session. A fresh
  cold-boot peer at the same floor continues to hold the readiness gate.
- **Held-peer clamp and F4 keep:** A kernel-upgrade hold advertises weight 0
  without changing local weight. A not-ready receiver may promote DEGRADED
  from that reported non-owner shape and may keep the RG over a ready peer
  after `ClearKernelUpgradeHold`; the hold does not automatically return
  ownership.
