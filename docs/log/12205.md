## STEP-0 (pre-rebase; original PR base `83b5c955335f16d8c7835f206b210ce3ae1a5fe5`)

At the original PR base, the production index refusal was already present and
returned before the bootstrap `.network`/`.link` writes, rename, and reload; the
lifeline identity record was written earlier. The direct #6789 and #7114
`setupBootstrapLifeline` fixtures covered index 0 only. No end-to-end index-1
or absent-index fixture existed at that base. This was a coverage-only change,
not a production correction.

After #12151 merged, `TestBootstrapNonIndexRemedyIsActionable12151` covered the
higher-index PCI and non-PCI/absent-index refusals end to end, asserting no
rename, reload, or `.network` file. It does not assert no `.link` or other file;
that file-creation assertion is the unique contribution of #12205.

## Change

`bootstrap_lifeline_index_12205_test.go` drives the real
`setupBootstrapLifeline` through two successful-observation cases:

- The default route names the second of two enumerated NICs (index 1).
- The default route names a NIC absent from the enumeration (index -1).

Both cells assert no `.network` or `.link`/other file, rename, or reload. They
intentionally do not assert zero writes or absence of the identity record:
production persists that record before the index refusals.

The index-1 cell kills the `idx < 0` mutant. On the current master tree, the
absent-index cell exits through the dedicated `idx == -1` refusal; `idx > 0`
alone is equivalent because that return remains in place. Its actual killing
mutant disables the `idx == -1` refusal and weakens the later `idx != 0` guard
to `idx > 0`. The index-0 complete-observation and first-rename controls ensure
that refusing everywhere does not pass.

The stale daemon README sentence claiming the refusal leaves “mutated NOTHING”
was narrowed to the accurate interface-state claim. No production source was
changed.
