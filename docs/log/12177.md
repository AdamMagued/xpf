# #12177 — refuse envelope-incompatible cuts without wedging rollback

- **Issue**: #12177 — staged envelope refusals could strand STAGED/PREFLIGHT or VERIFIED journals, and a same-tag replacement demoted the known-good predecessor before the final pre-STOP recheck.
- **Branch**: `fix/12177-campaign-pub`
- **Worktree**: `/var/worktrees/campaign-pub/wt-12177`
- **Base**: `83b5c955335f16d8c7835f206b210ce3ae1a5fe5`

## Why

A resumed staged downgrade refused by the PREFLIGHT envelope check could leave a STAGED/PREFLIGHT journal that blocked reruns and operator rollback. A rise in the live DB's reader floor detected at the pre-STOP recheck could likewise leave a VERIFIED journal behind. Separately, replacing the predecessor under the same version tag demoted its known-good stamp before that final recheck, so a refusal erased the safe rollback candidate.

## What

- Check the staged reader at INIT; clear stale journals on INIT/resumed-PREFLIGHT refusals and clear the journal on a pre-STOP envelope refusal, before any live mutation.
- Preserve a committed predecessor stamp across replacement only when every managed binary hash matches the pinned source. Different or unverifiable bytes are marked non-committed before replacement; a preserved stamp is demoted after the pre-STOP check passes.
- Cover fresh/resumed refusals, default and explicit rollback after pre-STOP refusal, byte-identical/different-byte replacement, failed-target exclusion, wrapped refusal classification, and crash-resumed replacement with an unverifiable predecessor binary.

## Risk / residual

The regression uses the package's fake system and temporary directories; no live cluster or systemd unit was exercised. A byte-identical predecessor remains eligible through a pre-STOP refusal; changed or unverifiable replacement bytes are excluded, and a cut that passes the gate marks the reused target in-flight before STOP. Refusal errors direct operators to re-stage an envelope-compatible target or run `xpfd upgrade --rollback` with `--target <compatible-version>` when default selection is unsuitable, after the refusal journal is clear.
