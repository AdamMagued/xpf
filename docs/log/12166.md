# #12166 — withhold bulk ack on transient session-install failures

- **Timestamp**: 2026-10-11 (`fix/12166-campaign-pub`)
- **Base**: rebased onto `a78c217fc`; #12160 (`56f62f1f3`) had landed via
  #12375.

## Why

The receive path added session keys to the authoritative bulk inventory before applying them. A transient helper transport failure could therefore leave rows missing while BulkEnd reconciled the incomplete snapshot, sent BulkAck, marked the bulk complete and released the HA hold. A later successful install cleared the helper warning but did not repair those missing rows. Semantic helper refusals remain an intentional-ACK path.

## What

- Track transient install failures per v4/v6 session for the active bulk,
  independently of the received-key set. Only the same key's successful
  install, definitive helper outcome, or successful delete clears its marker;
  failed deletes and unrelated successes do not. A new accepted bulk resets
  the per-family markers.
- Before stale reconciliation, `BulkEnd` checks both failure sets. Remaining
  transient failures withhold `BulkAck`, completion/hold-release callbacks,
  and reconciliation while the receive window stays open for an authoritative
  retry.
- The sender re-drives an aged unacknowledged forced-resync or authoritative
  bulk after `BulkAckPendingRetryAfter` (35s). A readiness latch survives
  retry source/write failures until a replacement bulk is pending or its
  matching `BulkAck` arrives. Forced-resync uses the DUE predicate; cold-prime
  keeps its separate AWAITED guard to avoid duplicate sends while its ack is
  in flight.
- Committed worker-repair results and known deterministic helper validation
  rejections are acknowledged without poisoning mirror health. Helper-down,
  `mirror-write-failed`, and unknown helper errors remain transient. Rebased
  the receive gate onto #12160's accepted-window serial and generation-keyed
  inventory contract; documented the behavior in `pkg/cluster/README.md`.

- Count typed deterministic helper rejections separately as
  `ImportsRejectedByHelper` and render the peer-session debt in cluster
  information; these rows are intentionally ACKed but were not installed.

## Residual / risk

The repair uses bounded full authoritative bulk retries, without a new wire
message or per-record protocol. Persistent helper transport failures keep the
receiver's bulk/readiness gate open and withhold acknowledgement rather than
promoting with a partial table. If retry snapshot acquisition fails, the
sender's retry-readiness debt remains latched; a replacement bulk keeps
readiness closed through its matching BulkAck. Semantic refusals remain
intentionally acked.
