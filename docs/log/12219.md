# 12219 — Retain unknown host-inbound children

## Boundary

`parseHostInboundNode` only consumed `system-services` and `protocols`, silently
losing other inner keywords. A compact unknown child such as
`host-inbound-traffic system-service ssh;` stays on the stanza's `Keys` tail
after normalization and was also dropped. Compact `apply-groups` heads were
also skipped, so a group stanza compiled with empty admission and no warning
while the braced form expanded normally. Strict Store commit and tolerant
load/sync therefore accepted zone typos without diagnostics. Per-interface
deny-all explanations also ignored physical-plus-unit override unions and
case-insensitive runtime service matching.

## Fix

- Retain unknown inner child keywords and unknown compact `Keys` heads,
  including surviving compact apply-meta statements, on `HostInboundTraffic`;
  valid packed `system-services` / `protocols` tails remain normalized and
  compile as before. Preserve unknown-child provenance through repeated-block
  merges and per-interface clones.
- Reject unknown zone-level children on strict compilation. Tolerant compilation
  and Store load/sync warn with the zone/interface and discarded keyword;
  `LoadOverride` followed by `Commit` and `SyncApply` cover the compact spelling.
- Explain per-interface deny-all only when the effective interface override has
  no recognized service or protocol. Use physical-plus-unit override resolution
  and case-folded known-token lookup, matching runtime admission.
