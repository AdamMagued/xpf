## Why

The authority tests were not reaching their intended session-hit behavior. Their WAN binding represents tagged logical interface `reth0.80`, but the generated Ethernet frames and descriptor metadata had no VLAN 80. The descriptor therefore dropped the packets as unknown VLAN. The ICMP test also seeded a forwarding decision inconsistent with the snapshot's HA ownership, connected routes, and neighbors, so unrelated forwarding resolution replaced the candidate decision.

## What

- Updated the test module's TCP and ICMP WAN packet fixtures to carry VLAN 80 in both the Ethernet frame and descriptor metadata (`ingress_vlan_id = 80`, `ingress_vlan_present = 1`), with frame offsets and packet length adjusted.
- Made the ICMP snapshot include the intended WAN/LAN redundancy-group ownership, LAN connected address, and static neighbors; seeded WAN egress now transmits through its physical parent.
- Changed only `userspace-dp/src/afxdp/tests_session_hit_authority_9519.rs`; production behavior is unchanged.
