## Product root cause (shared with #12496)

`poll_descriptor/filter.rs::revalidate_static_pbr_route_on_session_hit` treats
every forward non-tunneled `LocalDelivery` decision as requiring revalidation.
Its unchanged-local fast path covers the ordered ingress-primary-address and
interface-NAT local miss arms, but not a firewall address on a different
interface which the miss path resolves through the FIB local route. For the
DNAT target `10.0.61.1`, that is the relevant path: the translated target is
local on `reth1.0`, not on ingress `reth0.80`.

When the ordered local arms do not match, the old code bypassed the identity
fast path solely because the stored decision was `LocalDelivery`, then returned
a route revalidation even when the FIB returned the identical local resolution
under the identical route identity. `poll_descriptor/mod.rs` treats that
non-steer result as a discard, increments `filter_revoked_sessions`, and tears
down the session before the host-inbound and `junos-host` hit gates run.

A firsthand #12496 probe confirmed the same defect independently of tagged
VLANs: ingress is ifindex 24 (`reth1.0`), VLAN 0; the installed local session
had `(install identity 0,0)`. SYN: `local=1 create=1 sessions=1`. On ACK #0:
`session_hit=1 filter_revoked=1 sessions=0`, with no policy or host-inbound
deny. ACKs #1 and #2 were session misses. The stored resolution was
`LocalDelivery`, `tunnel_endpoint_id=0`; this is the same FIB-local route
revalidation, not #11297 VLAN-child handling.

The correction accepts the route result only when BOTH its full forwarding
resolution and its live route identity equal the stored decision. A changed
identity, lost local address, or changed resolution still returns the
fail-closed route transition. A new #12056 regression cell for a non-ingress
FIB-local address failed before this change and passes after it.

## Risk

The behavior change is limited to an established non-tunneled local-delivery
session whose live interface-NAT/FIB-local resolution and route identity are
unchanged. Address loss and route-identity changes remain fail-closed.
Operator-visible effect:
valid established DNAT-to-self ACKs reach the host-bound service and policy
gates instead of being discarded and having their session torn down. The
unrelated clean-base suite failures listed above remain outside scope.
