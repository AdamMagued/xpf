# Issue #12266 — slow-path residual broad-suite redness in NAT and delegated delivery

## Why

All 19 `tests_slow_path_disposition` failures named in #12266 were fixture defects. Three production changes tightened gates that these fixtures did not satisfy, so the cells stopped before reaching the paths they pin:

- **#11297** (`7d43c8346`) adds a VLAN parent (ifindex 11) and its configured VLAN child (`reth0.80`, ifindex 12) to `tagged_only_ingress_ifindexes`. `unknown_ingress_vlan` then drops VID-0 frames on either ifindex before Stage 11. The DNAT- and static-NAT-to-self IKE cells (`r1`–`r3`, `c1`, `c2`, `t5`–`t8`) sent untagged frames on ifindex 12. The GRE-inner IKE cells (`t1`–`t4`, `t9`, and the GRE halves of `t7` and `t8`) sent untagged outer frames on ifindex 11 without the native-VLAN selection (#11434) that admits VID 0 there.
- **#11439** (`84da38d6f`) limits the Stage-11 claim to `owns_ipsec_local_destination` with no transit-DNAT match. The two Stage-11 static-DNAT cells (`stage11_static_dnat_external_sa_miss_and_hit_10516`, `stage11_positive_cells_never_mint_adjudicated_q0_10516`) mapped their external address to a transit host.
- **#11074** (`72a825f4e`) checks the parked metadata's source against the transit-source martian gate on neighbor replay. The three neighbor-miss cells parked metadata with zero addresses.

## What

- Stage-11 static NAT: the external `203.0.113.10` now maps to the configured firewall address `192.168.1.1`. The test asserts that this target is XFRM-local and that the existing transit mapping is not.
- Neighbor-miss replay: the IPv4 and IPv6 cells stamp the wire addresses and ports into the parked metadata.
- `reth0.80` IKE frames: `tag_ike_frame_vlan80_10525` inserts the VLAN 80 tag ahead of the existing EtherType, so it works for IPv4 and IPv6. `tag_ike_meta_vlan80_10525` sets VID 80, `ingress_vlan_present = 1`, and +4 L3/L4/payload offsets, matching what the XDP shim reports for a tagged frame. The `#10525` helpers, `t5`, the NAT-T DNAT half of `t7`, and the v6 DNAT half of `t8` use both helpers.
- GRE-inner IKE: the IPv4 cells use `gre_to_self_native_untagged_snapshot()`. The v6 GRE half of `t8` sets native VLAN 80 on its ifindex-11 underlay row.
- The `#10525` comment and premise assertion now describe the Stage-11 claim as `owns_ipsec_local_destination` (#11439) instead of `owns_configured_ip`.

No production code changed. The VLAN, Stage-11 ownership, and replay martian gates are unchanged; the fixtures now satisfy them.

## Risk

Test-only. CI runs only `cargo check`, so these cells are guarded by local module runs, not CI. The `#10525` cells deliver on the VLAN child's own ifindex (12, VID 80); the parent-bind shape (ifindex 11, VID 80) is not exercised by this module. No live cluster was exercised.
