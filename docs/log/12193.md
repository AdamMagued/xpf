The round-1 synthesis rules SATURATE for PIO lifetimes over #12564's
proposed PIO-prune policy. The combined tree has REDS in either merge
order, with zero textual conflict: when saturation runs first, pruning
is dead and #12564's tests fail. #12564 retains its PREF64 arm and drops
