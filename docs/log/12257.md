## Why

Two `tests_fabric_zone_stamp` cells were red on master (#11649 triage):

- `unique_established_ri_snat_reply_is_admitted_without_stamp_11061`: on a
  routing-instance node the fabric gate admits an unstamped reply only when it
  has exactly one established forward-NAT match (#11061). The gate proved that
  match and moved the reply into the forward session's tenant domain, but
  session resolution then re-ran the domain-constrained reverse-NAT admission,
  which refuses the tenant candidate for a domain-zero wire reply. The reply
  never reached session authority (`session_hit` 0, expected 1).
- `foreign_fabric_stamp_cannot_serve_reverse_cache_10670`: the fixture owned
  its peer-imported reverse companion by the forward session's egress RG (2).
  Production owns a reverse companion by its client-side egress RG: reverse
  synthesis stamps `owner_rg_for_resolution`, the Go sync sender reports the
  entry's own FIB egress RG, RG activation refresh re-homes from the refreshed
  resolution, and #11373 re-homes on every generation-stale hit. Since #11373
  the first hit re-homes the import to LAN's locally active RG 1, so
  `cached_flow_decision_valid` invalidates the stamped descriptor and the
  cell's "real same-zone cache hit" precondition fails.

## What

- `poll_descriptor` hands the gate's `ForwardSessionMatch` to
  `resolve_flow_session_decision_with_conntrack` (new last argument; the
  test-only wrapper passes `None`). At consumption the resolver re-derives the
  proof: the domain-zero reply must still have a unique established local
  match equal to the proof, and the shared reverse alias must still be unique.
  Otherwise it returns `None` without installing. The reverse companion is
  keyed in the forward session's tenant domain.
- The 11061 fixture seeds the injected forward session's receiver-local policy
  verdict and SNAT provenance, so it exercises reply admission rather than the
  independent fail-closed stale-policy/stale-NAT gates. Its setup is now
  `established_ri_snat_forward_11061`, shared with two new resolver cells that
  pin the consumption recheck (stale local proof; missing or ambiguous shared
  alias).
- The 10670 cell owns the import by RG 1 and is re-scoped the way
  `stamped_fabric_punt_miss_is_judged_after_adjudicated_hit_10670` already
  is: it pins the stamped-zone authority decision and the cache preflight's
  side effects (the foreign stamp neither evicts the owner's descriptor nor
  counts as a hit), with a FIXTURE assertion that trips if a stamped reverse
  hit ever becomes servable. A topology whose reply RG is not locally active
  cannot host the cell: the owner ACK hits the import but does not forward
  (`tx` 0).
- NOT changed: owner-RG attribution. Stale re-resolution keeps master's block
  verbatim (#12074's policy re-judgement, then #11373's
  `owner_rg_for_resolution` re-home). `forwarding/ha.rs` is unchanged.

## Risk

No live cluster was exercised, and CI compiles Rust without running Rust
tests. The unstamped exception stays limited to one unique established local
and shared forward-NAT candidate, rechecked at consumption; other unstamped
fabric traffic keeps the fail-closed drop. A locally owned stamped reverse flow
is never served from the flow cache, so the 10670 cell no longer drives a
stamped reverse cache hit: the #10670 preflight's cache-serve protection is
pinned only through its no-evict and no-hit side effects and remains defense
in depth for owner states production does not keep. The base already carries
#12074 and #12255 (#12470), so no merge-order constraint remains.
