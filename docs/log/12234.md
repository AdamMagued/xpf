## Change

- `policyUnknownEnforcementChildren` quarantines every unknown direct policy
  child except a scalar one-edit `description` typo with no children and at
  most one value token. Packed typos carrying a scheduler binding, a
  `then deny`, or a second value token, and nested typo bodies, poison the
  policy.
- Skip Junos apply statements using `isApplyStatementKeyword` (#7029/#12217):
  group expansion honors `apply-groups-except`, and the compiler does not
  compile `apply-macro`; neither is dropped enforcement content.
- Keep strict unknown-child rejection (its message now says "unknown child")
  and the #4232 advisory, whose text now names which children quarantine.
  Add zone-pair poison/advisory rows, a global-policy poison row, and
  userspace snapshot cells for the packed/nested sentinel and the apply-group
  exclusion.
- The related known-`description` packed-tail issue is tracked separately as
  #12634 (F1b); it is not folded into this change.

## Residual / risk

Fail-closed quarantine rejects the whole userspace policy snapshot when an
unknown direct child is dropped; a fresh boot therefore remains default-deny
until the configuration is repaired. This includes legacy persisted policy
children other than the explicit description/apply exceptions. Mixed-version
HA peers can disagree during a rolling upgrade. No live-cluster mutation was
performed.
